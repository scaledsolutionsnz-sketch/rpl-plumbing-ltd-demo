Hot water

Your hot water system is the engine room of your home

It helps ensure your bathroom, kitchen and laundry perform well every day. From the invigorating morning shower to the heavy duty clothes wash, you need a system that meets the needs of your family.

Medium pressure cylinders are a step up from low pressure, giving stronger flow to showers and mixing taps. It is not mains pressure, but it is a great alternative without the cost of a full upgrade. These are vitreous enamel lined, which suits a greater range of water conditions, and the valves are upgraded to enable stronger pressure.

Mains pressure cylinders are normally glass lined steel. If your town supply pressure is not great, a mains pressure cylinder will not make a lot of difference. Often with mains pressure, if more than one tap is running there is very little change to water temperature.

Backflow and IQP

Protecting the drinking water supply

There is a risk to public health if there is a cross connection between your water supply and a contaminated source. If there is a pressure drop in the water main, a vacuum can be created, and under certain conditions this siphons liquid from a contaminated source back into the drinking water supply.

Water pressure can be affected by a break in the main, water being pumped during a fire, a customer using water at higher pressure than supplied, heavy use downstream, or an outlet sitting higher than the main.

Properties that pose a particular risk include chemical plants, pest controllers, market gardens, golf courses and sporting ovals, caravan parks, greywater treatment systems, metal processing plants, paint manufacturers, laundries, nurseries, and residential properties with rainwater storage.
