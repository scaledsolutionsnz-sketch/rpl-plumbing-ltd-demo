About us

A family plumbing firm, built on word of mouth

Rohland Plumbing Ltd — RPL Plumbing — is a family-run firm of NZ certified Master Plumbers. Founded by Chris Rohland in 2004, the business has been built job by job on honest pricing, tidy workmanship and turning up when we say we will.
